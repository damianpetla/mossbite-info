Mossbite — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026 · Applies to Mossbite 1.0.0

Mossbite is an offline arcade game developed by Damian Petla. This policy explains how the game and support communications handle information.

Information collected by the game

Mossbite does not collect or transmit personal information. It does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, in-app purchases, or player chat. The game does not access your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Local game data

Your best score is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device so the game can display it between sessions. It is not sent to the developer or shared with other players. It remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup is disabled for the current version. The developer cannot access or delete this local score remotely.

Children

Mossbite is intended for players aged six and older, including children. The same offline experience is provided to all players. The game does not collect personal information from children and does not contain advertising or social communication features.

Support communications

If you contact the developer by email, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please do not include sensitive information. Children should ask a parent or guardian to contact support on their behalf.

Support email is handled through Google's Gmail service and is subject to its processing and security practices. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request and any related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion of your support messages using the contact below. Support information is not sold or used for advertising.

Platform services and security

Google Play and Android may independently process information when you download or use apps under their own policies. Mossbite does not add its own telemetry or send game data to the developer. Local game data uses the app's private storage, and the current app does not request internet access.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. The effective date and applicable version will be updated on this page.
